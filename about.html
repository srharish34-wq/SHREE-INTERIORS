Our Foundation

A company built on trust and complete service

Shree Desire Groups was started in 2009 and has grown into a trusted name in real estate, infrastructure and sustainable development. We specialise in layout promoting, construction, interior and exterior design, and solar power plants, with RO water purifiers as an added service.

With a strong commitment to quality, innovation and customer satisfaction, we provide reliable, value-driven services for residential, commercial and industrial needs.

  • All-in-one solution, from plot purchase to delivery
  • User-friendly services and flexible budgets
  • Bank loan assistance available
